How it works: winner takes points from loser based on how surprising the win was. It adapts — new players move fast (K = 48 for the first 5 games, 32 until 20, then 24) so they find their level quickly, and a blowout counts a bit more than a deuce (up to 1.5× for 11–0). Names aren’t case-sensitive. Typo? Undo the latest game.
