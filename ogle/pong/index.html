Mechanism of action (how you are ranked)

Everyone starts at \(R_0 = 1000\). Each match (a single game or a best of 3) counts as one result. Before it, player \(A\)’s expected score against \(B\) is

$$E_A = \frac{1}{1 + 10^{(R_B - R_A)/400}}, \qquad E_B = 1 - E_A$$

The winner \(W\) takes points from the loser \(L\), with \(K = 40\) and the result rounded to the nearest 2:

$$\Delta = 2\cdot\operatorname{round}\!\left(\frac{K\,(1 - E_W)}{2}\right), \qquad R_W' = R_W + \Delta, \qquad R_L' = R_L - \Delta$$

So beating an equal player (\(E_W = \tfrac12\)) is \(\pm 20\), an upset is worth more, and a win you were expected to get is worth less. The margin doesn’t change the rating, and total points are conserved.
