Mechanism of action (how the rating works)

The winner takes points from the loser based on how surprising the win was. New players move fast (K = 48 for their first 5 games, 32 until 20, then 24) so they find their level quickly, and a blowout counts more than a deuce (up to 1.5× for 11–0). Names aren’t case-sensitive. Typo? Undo the latest game.
